Quick answer

The Pearl Izumi Elite Gel Cycling Gloves are our top pick for most riders, striking the best balance of comfort, breathability, and durability for everyday road and gravel riding.

The Giro Supernatural Road Cycling Gloves are a great runner-up for riders who prefer a minimalist, bar-close feel, while the Louis Garneau Biogel RX wins the Editor’s Pick for its exceptional moisture management and targeted padding.

Cycling gloves do more than just look the part. They protect your hands during a crash, absorb road vibration, and prevent the numbness that can creep in on long rides. A good pair also wicks sweat and gives you a reliable grip, especially in hot weather or when your hands get clammy. With so many options, full-finger vs. fingerless, gel vs. foam padding, varying cuff lengths, choosing the right glove can feel overwhelming. The key is to match the glove to your riding style and hand shape. Road cyclists often prefer minimal padding for better bar feel, while endurance riders and mountain bikers usually want more cushioning. Breathable mesh backs and moisture-wicking palms matter for warm-weather riding, and a secure closure keeps the glove from shifting.
